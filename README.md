# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, profitability, orders, product categories, payment methods, and regional sales trends.

The dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of business performance from **January 2023 to December 2024**, helping identify top-performing products, states, categories, and sales trends.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using **Power BI** to generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard allows users to monitor:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Quantity Sold
- Total Orders
- State-wise Sales
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Product Category Performance
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Payment Mode Distribution

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
2. Identify the states generating the highest sales.
3. Analyze monthly sales trends.
4. Identify top-performing products.
5. Compare sales across different product categories.
6. Understand customer payment preferences.
7. Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by State
A horizontal bar chart showing sales performance across different states.

**Top-performing states include:**
- Rajasthan
- Karnataka
- Gujarat
- Tamil Nadu
- Delhi
- Uttar Pradesh
- Maharashtra

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend
A line chart showing monthly sales performance from **2023–2024**.

This helps identify:
- Sales fluctuations
- Growth patterns
- High-performing months
- Low-performing periods

### 3. Sales by Product Category
A comparison of sales across major product categories such as:

- Electronics
- Clothing
- Home & Kitchen
- Beauty
- Sports

### 4. Top 10 Products by Sales
The dashboard highlights the products generating the highest sales.

Examples include:
- Tablet
- Smartphone
- Skin Cream
- Jeans
- Headphones
- Vacuum Cleaner
- Yoga Mat
- Makeup Kit
- Air Fryer
- Face Wash

### 5. Payment Mode Distribution
A donut chart showing the distribution of different payment methods:

- Cash on Delivery
- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- Net Banking
- UPI

The dashboard helps identify several important business insights:

Rajasthan is among the highest-performing states in terms of sales.
